RobotPay payment security

Protecting an account starts with careful access habits. Use a unique password and keep verification codes private.

Security checklist

  1. Choose a long, unique password for your account.
  2. Do not share your password or verification codes.
  3. Contact RobotPay if you notice activity you do not recognize.
