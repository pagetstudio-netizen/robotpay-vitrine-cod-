RobotPay multi-currency account

Keep funds in a local currency available in your account and prepare an international transfer using the recipient’s username.

How to prepare a transfer

  1. Choose the local-currency balance you want to use.
  2. Enter the recipient’s RobotPay username.
  3. Review the recipient and amount before confirming the request.
